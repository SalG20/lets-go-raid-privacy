Account and trainer information

We process your email address, account identifier, authentication/session records and trainer profile to provide account access and raid coordination. Your profile includes trainer name, trainer code, level, team, avatar choices, verification status and account preferences. Supabase provides authentication and our backend. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google authenticates you and supplies an identity token used to create your app session. Signup email checks may use ZeroBounce to detect invalid email addresses and reduce abuse.

Trainer details needed for raids are visible to the trainers with whom you coordinate. Hosted/joined raids, readiness, cancellations, reports, feedback and moderation records are processed to operate the service and prevent abuse.

If you publish a Social friend-code listing, other app users can see the trainer name, friend code, level, team, avatar, tags, country and Vivillon pattern included in your listing, and an online indicator when enabled. You can unpublish your listing. We store reward activity, points and game progress linked to your account to operate these features. Leaderboards can display your trainer name, rank and points or game results; you can change the Show me on leaderboard preference.

Raid Quick Messages let you choose predefined coordination messages. Supabase processes the chosen message identifier, sender account identifier, display name, role, timestamp and delivery/retry identifiers to send the message to authorized raid-room participants. The backend also stores send-throttle and retry information to prevent abuse and duplicate messages. Database broadcasts are stored in Supabase Realtime before automatic cleanup; they are not processed only in device memory or guaranteed to disappear immediately after delivery. Quick Message broadcasts, including your account identifier and display name, can remain in our provider's realtime message log for a limited period after delivery and are not removed immediately when you delete your account.

Verification screenshots are processed on your device using text recognition. The verification flow sends extracted trainer information, not the screenshot, to our backend.

Email signup uses a browser-based Cloudflare Turnstile security check. Our backend verifies the challenge token with Cloudflare and sends the email address to ZeroBounce for validation. Turnstile processes technical signals such as IP address, browser information and challenge context to detect bots; Cloudflare also uses these signals to improve its bot detection. See its Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

Location

Foreground location permission is optional. If you grant it, the app can obtain latitude and longitude, including precise coordinates when your device permits this, and reverse-geocode them to country, region and city. Host/boss-selection flows send coordinates and timezone/location context to backend functions that check raid availability and eligibility. Without permission, some location-dependent results may be unavailable.

Coarse country/region/city information and update timestamps may be stored with your profile. With existing location permission, the app also resolves a country code for operational activity reporting. Location access does not require background-location permission.

Our current backend uses precise coordinates to check raid eligibility and writes no exact coordinates into the raid record when creating a raid. The device location service may process coordinates to return a country, region or city. We do not promise that all infrastructure processing is memory-only or immediately erased. Service logs and backups have their own retention behavior. In version 1.0.7, Appodeal and BidMachine may also collect and share approximate or precise device location for advertising when you have granted the corresponding foreground location permission. You may deny or revoke this permission in Android settings. Advertising partners may infer approximate location from IP addresses even without location permission. Older versions using AdMob do not intentionally pass precise coordinates in the app ad-request code.

IV Scanner, Live Mode and screen capture

Screenshot scanning. When you choose a screenshot in the IV Scanner, the app reads it on your phone with on-device text recognition to identify the Pokémon and values such as CP, HP, level, size and appraisal. The screenshot and the values read from it are not sent to us or to advertising partners.

Live Mode (optional). Live Mode uses Android screen capture (MediaProjection). Android asks your permission every time you start it; on recent Android versions you can share one app (we recommend Pokémon GO only) or your entire screen. While Live Mode is on:

  • a notification with a Stop button is shown;
  • the app receives the shared screen continuously and keeps only the most recent image in memory;
  • it analyses the shared screen automatically when a new, steady screen appears (automatic scanning) and when you tap the floating eye button. If you share your entire screen, screens of other apps that you open may also be briefly analysed on your phone; anything that is not a Pokémon screen is discarded;
  • images are decoded and analysed on your phone. They are not uploaded, recorded as video, or sent to us or to third parties;
  • temporary images are stored in the app's private storage, replaced as new screens are analysed, and deleted when Live Mode stops;
  • the floating button and results are shown over other apps using Android's “Display over other apps” permission. No ads are shown over other apps.

You can stop Live Mode from the notification, the floating button menu, the Live Mode screen or Android's screen-sharing indicator. You can withdraw the overlay, notification or screen-capture permissions at any time in Android settings.

Text recognition. Text recognition uses Google ML Kit delivered through Google Play services and runs on your device. Google may receive technical information such as device and app information, performance metrics and error codes, as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure; image content is not sent.

Saved Pokémon. If you save a scanned Pokémon, its values, a reduced copy of the screenshot and a thumbnail are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, not on our servers, until you delete the scan or uninstall the app. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account.

Sharing, copying and saving images. Only when you choose to, the app creates an image of the results panel and can share it through the Android share sheet, copy it to the clipboard, or save it to your phone's Pictures/Let's Go Raid folder, where other apps with photo access can see it. The app writes to the clipboard but never reads it.

Pokémon images. Pokémon images are loaded when needed from public image repositories hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). These requests contain no account information, but the host receives standard technical data such as your IP address and which image is requested.

Onboarding questions and time zone

After you create an account (or once, for existing accounts), the app may ask “How did you hear about us?”. Answering is optional and you can skip it. The choices are Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Friend or Family, App Store and Other. If you choose Other, you may voluntarily type a short description (up to 80 characters). We store the option you selected, any text you typed, and whether you answered or skipped the question, linked to your account. We use this information to understand how people discover the app and to improve our marketing. It is not shown to other trainers, and our app does not send it to advertising providers.

The app reads the time zone configured on your device (for example Europe/Madrid). As an initial suggestion it may also use the country that time zone belongs to and the region from your device language/region settings. Time-zone detection uses device settings only; it does not use GPS and does not request location permission. During onboarding you may optionally search an offline list of cities and countries and choose one; the search runs on your device. We store your time zone as an IANA time-zone identifier, the country you selected or that was suggested, the city and region if you chose a city, and when you last confirmed your time zone. In Settings > Time Zone you can update it to the time zone currently set on your device; a change of the time zone used for raid hosting is possible once every 24 hours.

We use this information to show your time-zone preference in your settings and to check which raid bosses with local-time or regional availability you can host (these checks use your confirmed time zone and server time). A device time zone or a city you choose is not verified and is not treated as proof of your physical location. This information is not shown to other trainers, and our app does not send it to advertising providers.

These answers are stored in our backend (Supabase) and a copy is kept on your device so the app can show them offline. Only you, through the app, and the app operator can access them. They are kept while your account exists and are deleted together with your account (Settings > Delete Account). You can contact us to access or correct them.

Advertising and privacy choices

The advertising SDKs in version 1.0.7 include Appodeal, BidMachine, Bidon, AppLovin, Mintegral, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), Meta Audience Network and Pangle; Sentry supports SDK crash reporting. Appodeal collects ad interactions, advertising and other identifiers, technical device/network information, diagnostics and other app performance data for advertising and analytics. Mediated advertising SDKs may also process these data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention; practices vary by provider. Data may be shared with advertising and measurement partners. Permissions and consent choices affect collection; declining personalized ads does not necessarily stop all technical data collection. For rewarded ads, our app passes an opaque reward identifier to Appodeal to verify rewards and help prevent abuse. Our backend links this identifier to your account; it is pseudonymous, not anonymous. It is not your raw account ID, email address or trainer name. The app does not directly send purchase events to Appodeal. This does not exclude collection by other bundled SDKs: Unity Ads documents collection and sharing of personal identifiers for app functionality and purchase history for advertising and analytics. Google Play and RevenueCat process purchases as described below.

Version 1.0.7 uses Appodeal mediation for banner ads and optional rewarded ads for temporary feature access. Rewarded ads do not grant Premium or lifetime Remove Ads. Ad-free access hides banner placements. No interstitial or app-open format is implemented. Older installed versions may use Google AdMob for rewarded ads with banners disabled.

In older installed versions using AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising/app-set or other device identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners process this information under their policies. Non-personalized ads can still involve identifiers, storage and measurement.

Version 1.0.7 uses Appodeal Stack Consent Manager during SDK initialization to obtain privacy choices where required. Older installed versions using AdMob use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where required, Settings provides Ad privacy choices to reopen the privacy form. Availability depends on your region and the consent platform's requirements. Device settings also let you manage advertising identifiers. Declining optional rewarded ads does not delete your account or prevent ordinary raid coordination.

Purchases

Monthly Premium (monthly_premium) and the separate Remove Ads purchase (remove_ads) are available through Google Play Billing. RevenueCat processes account/customer identifiers, purchase history, subscription status and entitlements for validation, restoration and purchase analytics. Our backend receives billing events to keep access current. We do not receive full payment-card details. Subscriptions and refunds are managed through Google Play. Deleting an app account does not itself cancel a Google Play subscription.

Notifications and operational data

If you enable notifications, Expo and Google/Firebase notification services process push tokens, device/installation identifiers, platform information and delivery data. Our backend stores registration and preference information and sends raid/service notifications. You can disable notifications in Settings or Android's notification settings.

We process app/build version, operating system, device model, session identifiers, screen/activity events and error summaries for reliability, analytics, support and abuse prevention. Activity reporting is linked to your app account. Advertising SDK diagnostics are separate from our operational reporting.

Device permissions. Depending on the features you use, the app may ask for: notifications; foreground location (host eligibility and coarse country/region); photo access on older Android versions (choosing screenshots); screen capture and display over other apps (Live Mode). Each is optional and can be withdrawn in Android settings; related features then stop working.

Retention and account deletion

Account/profile information is retained while your account is active. Raid, moderation, operational and support records may be retained for service operation, safety and troubleshooting. Billing records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies and legal obligations. Backups and service logs are not necessarily erased immediately when you delete an account.

You can request account deletion in Settings > Delete Account and complete the confirmation. You can also email peacefulsoil4k@gmail.com from your account email with the subject "Account Deletion Request - Let's Go Raid"; include your trainer name so we can verify ownership. You do not need to reinstall the app to send this request.

Account deletion removes the authentication account and profile and anonymizes associated raid/member display information. Some raid and moderation records remain after the identity fields described above are removed. Retained backend billing events can still contain account/customer identifiers and provider event payloads; these records are not necessarily anonymous. Verification screenshots are not held by our backend. Deleting your account does not erase records independently held by Google, RevenueCat, Appodeal and its advertising partners or other providers, and does not cancel a subscription. Pokémon you saved in the IV Scanner are stored only on your phone and are removed when you delete them in the IV Scanner or uninstall the app; deleting your account does not remove them from your phone.

There is currently no established separate process for deleting selected app data while keeping the account. You can contact support about access, correction or other privacy concerns; this does not promise selective deletion of every record.
