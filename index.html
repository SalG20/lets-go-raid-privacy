Privacy Policy

Let's Go Raid is operated by Peaceful Soil Studio. Android package: com.peacefulsoil.letsgoraid. Contact: peacefulsoil4k@gmail.com. Public policy: https://salg20.github.io/lets-go-raid-privacy/

Account and trainer information

We process your email address, account identifier, authentication/session records and trainer profile to provide account access and raid coordination. Your profile includes trainer name, trainer code, level, team, avatar choices, verification status and account preferences. Supabase provides authentication and our backend. If you choose Google Sign-In, Google authenticates you and supplies an identity token used to create your app session. Signup email checks may use ZeroBounce to detect invalid email addresses and reduce abuse.

Trainer details needed for raids are visible to the trainers with whom you coordinate. Hosted/joined raids, readiness, cancellations, reports, feedback and moderation records are processed to operate the service and prevent abuse.

Verification screenshots are processed on your device using text recognition. The verification flow sends extracted trainer information, not the screenshot, to our backend.

Location

Foreground location permission is optional. If you grant it, the app can obtain latitude and longitude, including precise coordinates when your device permits this, and reverse-geocode them to country, region and city. Host/boss-selection flows send coordinates and timezone/location context to backend functions that check raid availability and eligibility. Without permission, some location-dependent results may be unavailable.

Coarse country/region/city information and update timestamps may be stored with your profile. With existing location permission, the app also resolves a country code for operational activity reporting. Location access does not require background-location permission.

Our current backend uses precise coordinates to check raid eligibility and writes no exact coordinates into the raid record when creating a raid. The device location service may process coordinates to return a country, region or city. We do not promise that all infrastructure processing is memory-only or immediately erased. Service logs and backups have their own retention behavior. Precise coordinates are not intentionally provided to AdMob by our ad-request code; Google may infer approximate location from IP addresses independently.

Advertising and privacy choices

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads for temporary feature access. Rewarded ads do not grant Premium or lifetime Remove Ads. Production banner ads are currently disabled. No interstitial or app-open format is implemented.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK processes IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising/app-set or other device identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners process this information under their policies. Non-personalized ads can still involve identifiers, storage and measurement.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain privacy choices where required and gates ad requests on its consent state. Where required, Settings provides Ad privacy choices to reopen the privacy form. Availability depends on your region and the consent platform's requirements. Device settings also let you manage advertising identifiers. Declining optional rewarded ads does not delete your account or prevent ordinary raid coordination.

Purchases

Monthly Premium (monthly_premium) and the separate Remove Ads purchase (remove_ads) are available through Google Play Billing. RevenueCat processes account/customer identifiers, purchase history, subscription status and entitlements for validation, restoration and purchase analytics. Our backend receives billing events to keep access current. We do not receive full payment-card details. Subscriptions and refunds are managed through Google Play. Deleting an app account does not itself cancel a Google Play subscription.

Notifications and operational data

If you enable notifications, Expo and Google/Firebase notification services process push tokens, device/installation identifiers, platform information and delivery data. Our backend stores registration and preference information and sends raid/service notifications. You can disable notifications in Settings or Android's notification settings.

We process app/build version, operating system, device model, session identifiers, screen/activity events and error summaries for reliability, analytics, support and abuse prevention. Activity reporting is linked to your app account. Advertising SDK diagnostics are separate from our operational reporting.

Providers and disclosures

Providers include Supabase (https://supabase.com/privacy), Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing, Google/Firebase services and AdMob (https://policies.google.com/privacy), RevenueCat (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy), Expo (https://expo.dev/privacy), and signup email-validation provider ZeroBounce (https://www.zerobounce.net/privacy-policy.html).

We use providers to operate the app, authenticate users, deliver notifications, validate purchases, serve ads and protect the service. We may disclose information to comply with legal obligations or investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention and account deletion

Account/profile information is retained while your account is active. Raid, moderation, operational and support records may be retained for service operation, safety and troubleshooting. Billing records may be retained by Google Play and RevenueCat under their own policies and legal obligations. Backups and service logs are not necessarily erased immediately when you delete an account.

You can request account deletion in Settings > Delete Account and complete the confirmation. You can also email peacefulsoil4k@gmail.com from your account email with the subject "Account Deletion Request - Let's Go Raid"; include your trainer name so we can verify ownership. You do not need to reinstall the app to send this request.

Account deletion removes the authentication account and profile and anonymizes associated raid/member display information. Some anonymized raid, moderation and billing records can remain. Verification screenshots are not held by our backend. Deleting your account does not erase records independently held by Google, RevenueCat or other providers, or cancel a subscription.

There is currently no established separate process for deleting selected app data while keeping the account. You can contact support about access, correction or other privacy concerns; this does not promise selective deletion of every record.

Children, security and contact

The app is not directed to children under 13 or below the applicable minimum age in their region. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information improperly. We use HTTPS for app/backend communication, but cannot guarantee absolute security. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Contact peacefulsoil4k@gmail.com to make a request.

We will update this policy when our practices change. Let's Go Raid is independent of Niantic, Nintendo and The Pokémon Company.
